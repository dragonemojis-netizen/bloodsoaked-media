Nobody expected Darkest Hour to sound this heavy under those lights.

The Sydney Opera House isn't a metal venue. It's marble and policy and tourists who don't know what blast beats are. Darkest Hour walked on stage anyway and played like they were trying to crack the foundation.

## The set

*Doomsayer* material sat next to older Thrash-influenced work. The crowd was small by festival standards but loud enough to make up for it. Paul Marshall's vocals cut clean through a mix that actually worked — rare for a support slot in a hall this big.

## Why it mattered

Shows like this are why I started writing. Not the arena spectacle — the mismatch. Metal in a place that wasn't ready for it. That friction is the whole story.

I filed this for Metal Lifestyle the week after the show. Still remember the ringing.
