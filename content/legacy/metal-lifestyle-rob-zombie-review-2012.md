Rob Zombie has been making the same record in different costumes for years now, and *The Lords of Salem* is no exception. That isn't an insult. When you build a career on B-movie horror and factory-floor riffs, consistency is a feature.

## What works

The production is thick — drum machines, sampled voices, that industrial grind he never fully abandoned after the White Zombie days. "Dead City" and "The Lords of Salem" hit like a midnight radio broadcast from a station that shouldn't exist. If you came for atmosphere, you get atmosphere.

## What doesn't

Lyrically, it's the same carnival Zombie has run since the nineties. If you need evolution, look elsewhere. This is comfort food for people who still own the *Hellbilly Deluxe* cassette.

## Verdict

For fans, essential. For everyone else, you've heard this movie before — but the lighting is still good.
