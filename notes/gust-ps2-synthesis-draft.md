## notes

I didn't really plan on collecting Gust's PS2 games. I bought Ar tonelico on a whim and ended up loving the shit out of it, and from there I started picking up the other games from this period. Atelier Iris, Ar tonelico, Mana Khemia, they're all doing pretty different things, but you can still feel that they're coming from the same place. I really like this era of Gust because they were just making whatever weird JRPG they wanted to make, and I wanted to have that part of their history on my shelf. I don't have everything they made, and I don't really need to. These are just the games from that era that I ended up caring about.
